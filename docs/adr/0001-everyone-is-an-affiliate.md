# 0001 — Everyone who comes in is an affiliate with a 3-referral monthly quota

Status: accepted (Justin Bellware, 2026-10-08)

Every person who comes into the CRM is an affiliate. Each affiliate has a quota of 3 referrals per month. Referrals must be attributed to a person. Justin can message the agent to credit a referral to someone.

The agent keeps views (or lists) of: people who have not referred this month; people who still owe referrals this month; people who are prime for upsells. Upsell tagging covers two products: the Revenue Partnerships program and the AI Guy Mastermind.
